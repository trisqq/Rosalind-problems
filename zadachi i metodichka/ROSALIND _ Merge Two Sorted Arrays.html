Problem

The merging procedure is an essential part of “Merge Sort” (which is considered in one of the next problems).

Given: A positive integer n≤105n≤105 and a sorted array A[1..n]A[1..n] of integers from −105−105 to 105105, a positive integer m≤105m≤105 and a sorted array B[1..m]B[1..m] of integers from −105−105 to 105105.

Return: A sorted array C[1..n+m]C[1..n+m] containing all the elements of AA and BB.

Sample Dataset

4
2 4 10 18
3
-5 11 12

Sample Output

-5 2 4 10 11 12 18

Hintclick to expand

The very first element of CC is either A[1]A[1] or B[1]B[1], whichever is smaller. The rest of CC can then be constructed recursively.

Source: Algorithms by Dasgupta, Papadimitriou, Vazirani. McGraw-Hill. 2006.
